Waddle

Domains: Reinforcement Learning Bipedal Locomotion

Unlike humans, two-legged robots lack natural instincts. They rely on an agent that learns balance like a video game loop: observing its current state, taking an action, and earning a reward for staying upright. In our project, we built this decision-making model to achieve actual walking.
