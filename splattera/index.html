Splattera

Domains: 3D Reconstruction Gaussian Splatting Computer Vision

Splaterra is a 3D reconstruction project focused on scalable and optimized reconstruction over long-context trajectories, transforming campus walkthrough videos into photorealistic, interactive 3D environments using Gaussian Splatting, camera pose estimation, and depth prediction. It focuses on maintaining consistent reconstruction quality across long trajectories, enabling applications in robotics, autonomous navigation, embodied AI, and digital twins.
