PlatyPulse

Domains: Embedded Systems PCB Design Communication

PlatyPulse is a custom-built handheld wireless experimentation platform inspired by the Flipper Zero. Built around the ESP32S3, it brings subGHz RF, RFID/NFC, and IR capabilities together into a single compact system.

PlatyPulse is designed to capture, analyze, store, and transmit supported wireless signals, giving you one platform to experiment with different forms of wireless communication. From exploring 433 MHz RF signals and reading NFC tags to learning and replaying IR commands, it turns wireless protocols into something you can actively experiment with.

The project brings together custom PCB design, embedded firmware, wireless communication, signal processing, and hardware integration in one device, making PlatyPulse a pocket-sized playground for learning, prototyping, and wireless experimentation.
