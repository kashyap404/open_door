What SRA is

Society of Robotics And Automation (SRA) is a student society at VJTI that deals with robotics, machine vision, automation and allied fields.

SRA is a space for learning, building, and sharing.

Open Door

Every year, SRA hosts “Open Door”, where first years get the opportunity to explore and learn about Projects built by their seniors, giving them useful insights into different domains and discover where their interest lies.

Workshops at SRA

SRA conducts four structured workshops for first-year students, helping them learn, build, and explore different domains of robotics.

Inception is an introductory seminar conducted by SRA for the first year students to prepare them for the world of robotics and automation. It covers the basics of Electronics, IOT, Mechanics and much more and acts as prerequisite knowledge for the upcoming workshops.

Inception 2025

Wall-E focuses on the concepts of line-following and self-balancing, using ESP32, a powerful micro-controller with features like inbuilt Wi-Fi support and BLE, with extensive application in several domains like IoT, RF, etc. The workshop entails various essential concepts such as PID Control, Embedded Communication Protocols, PWM, Filters, RTOS, etc. and gives its attendees (first-year students) a general idea about the world of robotics and embedded hardware.

Pixels is a dive into the field of Computer Vision. The workshop is designed to give the participants a basic understanding of the field of Computer Vision and its applications. It starts teaching students the very basics of Python and git, and than moves towards image processing and computer vision using the OpenCV library. Students also get to design their very own blob detection which will help track objects in videos or live camera.

In the MARIO workshop freshers design and implement a robotic manipulator(4-DOF Manipulator with Gripper on ROS Based IO) using intricate concepts of forward and inverse kinematics, with Robot Operating System (ROS).
