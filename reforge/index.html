Reforge

Domains: FPGA PCB Design Embedded Systems

Reforge is a custom FPGA development board designed around the Lattice iCE40 FPGA and ESP32-WROOM-32E, bringing programmable digital logic and embedded control together on a single board. The project aims to provide a practical platform for exploring FPGA-based digital systems while gaining hands-on experience in hardware and firmware development.
