AmazeDex

Domains: Reinforcement Learning Dexterous Manipulation MuJoCo

AmazeDex is a reinforcement learning project focused on autonomous in-hand dexterous manipulation using a 4-fingered Amazing Hand by Pollen Robotics. The system trains control policies in MuJoCo simulation to reorient a cube to target poses, using visual tracking to deploy the learned movements onto real hardware.

Approach / Workflow

  1. Model the robotic hand and environment in MuJoCo

    Define the 8-DOF hand, actuators, joints, collision geometry, cube, and environment, then establish a controlled simulation for safe reinforcement learning experiments.

  2. Explore reinforcement learning strategies

    Evaluate PPO, curriculum learning, and DDPG with HER, identifying issues such as reward hacking, local minima, and poor convergence before adopting SAC for manipulation.

  3. Design the reward and training environment

    Shape rewards around reaching, pushing, rotation, and successful orientation while modifying cube geometry, restricting initial conditions, and introducing domain randomization for robust policy learning.

  4. Train and evaluate the SAC policy

    Use a large replay buffer and entropy-driven exploration to learn dynamic finger coordination, achieving approximately 80% accuracy under fixed simulation conditions and around 70% with domain randomization.

  5. Deploy through a perception-to-control pipeline

    Estimate cube pose from camera observations using AprilTags, feed the resulting state into the trained SAC policy, and convert predicted joint actions into physical servo commands.

  6. Analyze the sim-to-real gap

    Compare simulation and hardware behavior, accounting for servo latency, backlash, friction, calibration offsets, and perception errors, while identifying digital-twin and improved perception methods for future iterations.

Key Concepts

Deep Reinforcement Learning

Training a robotic hand through trial and error so it learns coordinated finger movements for dexterous cube manipulation without explicitly programmed motion sequences.

Soft Actor-Critic (SAC)

An off-policy reinforcement learning algorithm that combines experience replay and entropy regularization, enabling exploration and effective reuse of successful manipulation trajectories.

Reward Engineering

Designing rewards that encourage reaching, pushing, rotation, and target orientation while preventing the policy from exploiting easy strategies such as simply holding the cube.

MuJoCo Simulation

A physics-based simulation environment used to safely train and evaluate the robotic hand before deploying learned manipulation policies onto physical hardware.

Sim-to-Real Transfer

Bridging differences between simulated and physical environments through domain randomization, calibrated hardware models, robust perception, and compensation for actuator and mechanical discrepancies.

Visual Pose Estimation

Estimating the cube's position and orientation from camera observations using approaches such as AprilTags, replacing direct simulator state access during physical deployment.
