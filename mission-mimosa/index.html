Mission Mimosa

Domains: Tactile Sensing Vision Robot Learning

Mission Mimosa explores giving robots a sense of touch, enabling them to go beyond simply seeing the world. By combining vision, tactile sensing, and robot learning, we aim to help robots understand physical interactions and manipulate objects more effectively, bringing them a step closer to interacting with the real world like humans do.
