TinyGPU

Domains: GPU FPGA Vector processor ASIC design

TinyGPU is a custom Risc-V Vector Extension supporting matrix multiplication accelerator. It acts as a hardware accelerator to CVA6 CPU. We have used SIMD style architecture to design and implement the GPU on a Zynq-zc702 FPGA and have observed a speed up of 16x for 4x4 matmul.

As demand for LLMs and graphics goes up this was a good project to learn why accelerators are needed and how to design them from a hardware point of view. For complete info, click here.
