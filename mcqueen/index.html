McQueen

Domains: Autonomous Driving Computer Vision Edge Computing

McQueen is a vision-based autonomous RC car using a single RGB camera for perception. A Jetson Nano streams camera data to a remote NVIDIA RTX 4090, where a pretrained spatiotemporal driving backbone and McQueen-specific lightweight action head predict steering and throttle. Commands return to the car for real-time, closed-loop control.
