FLEXWALK

Domains: Mechanical Design Control Systems Kinematics

A humanoid bipedal robot built from the ground up, with hip pitch, hip roll, knee pitch, and ankle pitch joints. We CAD-designed and built the entire mechanism, then use closed-loop control to coordinate its movements and bring it to life.

We’re working on making its movements more stable, precise, and human-like, while developing the control and gait-generation systems that allow it to walk. FlexWalk brings together mechanical design, electronics, kinematics, and control into one integrated platform.
